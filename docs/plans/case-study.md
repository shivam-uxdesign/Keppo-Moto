# Portfolio case study: Keppo Moto (plan, 25 Nov)

Asked: "A full on interactive case study with motions and clean transitions, and a quick TL;DR version of it" for the rider's design portfolio, "as an AI-first hands-on designer who can deliver products".

## Decided
- **Audience:** startup product roles (never named on the page; rider, 25 Nov: "Don't mention founding designer"): someone who works independently without a big team, creative, a problem solver.
- **Angle:** AI-first, up front. The designer owns vision, design, decisions and ride testing; the AI builds, researches, tests and ships builds.
- **Look:** Keppo Moto's design system (dark, teal, Geist / Bricolage, "instrument cluster at night").
- **Length:** full case study about 8–10 minutes; TL;DR about 1 minute (one screen, plus a one-page PDF).
- **Where:** the rider's portfolio project on this account, published on Vercel; exact place to be told later.

## Structure (full)
1. Hero: phone on a bike mount, the route drawing itself, one line on what was built.
2. The product: every ride, kept (rides, moments, safety, Studio Reels).
3. My role: what the designer did vs what the AI did.
4. The loop: idea → discuss → prototype → build → APK → ride → feedback (animated, real cycle times).
5. Deep dive, Keep moving: "a route with zero traffic" → step 0 reality check → Google's terms and costs → three modes → "No foot down" detours → traffic memory → the crash found on a ride and fixed.
6. Deep dive, the directions screen: the screenshot with 9 problems → options A, B, C → C2 → shipped (before / after slider; "nothing behind a tap", "no voice").
7. Shorter cards: Studio Reels, the HUD, the design system, safety.
8. Decisions and trade-offs: cost vs quality, Google's rules, what was not built (the paid server).
9. What I learned designing with AI.
10. Close: contact, links.

Motion: sticky phone that swaps screens on scroll, route line drawing, before / after slider, build timeline, counters, an embedded mini prototype, quiet fades and lifts, reduced-motion support, phone-friendly.

Real numbers only, from the repo: the plan log (studio-feature-plan.md, ~148 rows), the build count (versionCode ≈ commits, ~474), prototypes, design system, docs.
