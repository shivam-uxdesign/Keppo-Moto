## Why I ride, and why I built this

I ride because a motorcycle never lets you pretend. Summer cooks you, winter bites, the rain
finds every gap in your jacket, and the first time you lean into a corner every instinct tells
you to stay upright. You lean anyway, and on the day it goes wrong, you get back on. I built
Keppo Moto because riding was the best part of my week and my phone remembered none of it: not
how far I leaned, not the moment I wanted to keep, and nobody would know if I didn't get back up.
