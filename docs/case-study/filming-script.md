# Tomorrow's 5-minute ride: what to set up and what to say

## Before you leave (parked, away from home)
- Profile → Developer: **Demo mode OFF**.
- Home: **Moments ON**, **Film when I speak ON**, **Keep moving ON**.
- Phone screen recorder: sound set to **None** (Keppo needs the mic to hear you; your voice
  lands in the Moments clips instead, which can also become the Reel for R4).
- Start the screen recording, *then* open Keppo Moto. Start somewhere that isn't near home.

Speak naturally, in short bursts. Every time you talk, Keppo films a moment, so each line
below becomes a clip. Never touch the phone while moving.

## 0:00 – 0:45 · Parked: slide, Where to?, plan, Start (R2 / 14–16)
> "This is Keppo Moto. One slide and it asks where I'm going."
> (Pick a destination, not a saved Home place.)
> "Three ways to get there. Normal is Google's fastest. Less traffic picks the clearest road.
> No foot down takes the long way so I barely stop."
> (Pick Less traffic or No foot down, press Start.)

## 0:45 – 2:00 · Rolling: the directions dock (17)
> "Google's turn-by-turn up top, and my ride underneath: speed, lean, the clip button.
> I never have to leave the directions."

## 2:00 – 3:00 · Filming while navigating (17b)
> "Watch this: I'm just talking, and it's already filming. No buttons, no gloves off."
> One line about the road right now: the light, the traffic, how the bike feels.

## 3:00 – 4:00 · If traffic shows up (17c / 17d)
- Amber jam warning: "There's the jam warning, before I'm sitting in it."
- Clearer-road offer: "And it's offering me a clearer road. One tap." (Only when stopped.)
- If neither appears, say what you'd normally do in this traffic. That's still useful.

## 4:00 – 5:00 · Why it matters (pull-quote material)
> "Summer cooks you, winter bites, the rain gets in anyway, and you still get back on.
> This app remembers the ride, and it's watching out for the day I don't get back up."

## After
- End the ride, stop the recording, plug the phone in, and send the recording here.
- I'll run `docs/case-study/cut-recording.py sheet` to find the moments, cut 17–17d and R2, blur
  anything personal, and show you before committing.
