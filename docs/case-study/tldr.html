TL;DR · one minute

A motorcycle app, designed and shipped by one designer with AI as the engineering team.

The idea: the phone already on a rider's handlebar can do the jobs of a riding crew. Keppo Moto records every ride and lean angle, films the moments worth keeping by itself, texts your people if you crash, floats a HUD over Maps, turns clips and your own words into Reels, and steers you around Delhi traffic. I owned the idea, product, design and ride testing; AI wrote, tested and built the code.

  • 01
    Problem solving with my own data.

    "Yellow on Google is a 1-in-5 jam" became the scoring rule for Keep moving, a route that avoids jams. Later it learns each rider's own number.

  • 02
    Rules a riding app needs.

    Nothing behind a tap, no voice, no vibration. The directions screen went from "very bad" to redesigned and shipped in a day, via five prototypes.

  • 03
    Cost and terms as design inputs.

    No paid server; warnings use free data and the ₹1.3 route request is the last resort. Google's rules shaped which map each screen uses.

  • 04
    Tested on the road.

    A crash found mid-ride was fixed and rebuilt the same day. I installed and rode every build myself.
